# Credits

Atelier is original writing and code, built on ideas from the work below. It also bundles files from eight of these projects, each pinned to a commit with its licence (see "Vendored files" below), and open-licensed fonts. Where a short phrase is quoted, it is attributed in the text. All listed licences were checked in October 2026, and lemo-opuscar's on 1 November 2026.

## Creative method behind the composer
Edward de Bono (lateral thinking: provocation, random entry, reversal). Genrich Altshuller (TRIZ inventive principles). Bob Eberle (SCAMPER, 1971). Arthur Koestler (bisociation, The Act of Creation, 1964). Oulipo (writing under constraint). Liz Lerman (Critical Response Process). Ed Catmull (Pixar's Braintrust, Creativity, Inc., 2014). Jean-Baptiste Mouret and Jeff Clune (MAP-Elites, 2015). Lois Valdez and Albert Mehrabian (colour and emotion, 1994). Domicele Jonauskaite and colleagues (colour-emotion associations in 30 nations, 2020). Björn Ottosson (OKLab, 2020). The operators, lenses, formats and rubrics are original writing.
