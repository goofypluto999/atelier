What does a Friday release checklist really need?

The WHO Surgical Safety Checklist stops a surgical team three times: sign in before anaesthesia (7 checks), time out before the first incision (7 checks), and sign out before the patient leaves the room (5 checks). In 8 hospitals that used it, deaths fell from 1.5% to 0.8%: 15 in every 1,000 operations became 8 (Haynes et al., NEJM 2009).

A Friday release can take the same three stops, each with its own short list. Before the change: is the rollout staged? At go-live: are the faults watched, so a problem shows quickly? Before you leave: has the rollback been tried? Those three answers are the Google SRE book's: progressive rollouts, finding problems quickly, rolling back safely.

Sources: WHO Surgical Safety Checklist (2009). Haynes et al., NEJM 2009;360:491-499. Site Reliability Engineering (Google, O'Reilly 2016), Introduction.
