What does a Friday release checklist really need?

The WHO Surgical Safety Checklist stops a surgical team three times: sign in before anaesthesia (7 checks), time out before the first incision (7), and sign out before the patient leaves the room (5). In 8 hospitals that used it, deaths fell from 1.5% to 0.8%: 15 in every 1,000 operations became 8.

A Friday release can take the same three stops: before the change, at go-live, and before you leave. Each stop gets its own short list.

Sources: WHO Surgical Safety Checklist (2009). Haynes et al., NEJM 2009;360:491-499.
