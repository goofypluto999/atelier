What does a Friday release checklist really need?

The WHO Surgical Safety Checklist has 19 checks. That is far past the 5 to 9 items Atul Gawande says one pause can hold. It works anyway, because it never asks for more than seven at once: sign in before anaesthesia (7), time out before the first incision (7), sign out before the patient leaves the room (5). In 8 hospitals, deaths fell from 1.5% to 0.8% with it.

A Friday release can stop three times too: before the change, at go-live, and before you leave. Each stop gets its own short list, nine at most.

Sources: WHO Surgical Safety Checklist (2009). Atul Gawande, The Checklist Manifesto (2009), ch. 6. Haynes et al., NEJM 2009;360:491-499.
