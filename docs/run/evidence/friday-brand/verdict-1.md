1. page-01, the ceiling (the crimson "9" at about x 795-832, y 565-615, and the 12 px crimson bar at x 812-824): the thing the post turns on is the smallest, last-read mark. attention.py ranks the accent last (0.7%), under the fine print (3.5%), and the "9" is set at 64 px beside a 128 px headline. The viewer sees "7, 7, 5" and has to find the limit those numbers sit under. -> Make the ceiling the loud mark of the chart: set the "9" at headline size or close to it, thicken the bar to about 24 px, and keep the accent on those two marks only. (Rule: measured rank. Judgement: how big.)
2. page-01, chart block (x 80-832, y 665-880) and headline (y 105-450): craft slips. The counts ride on the labels at ragged x (the "7" ends at 272 for "Sign in", 322 for "Time out", 308 for "Sign out"), so the gap to the first box is 88, 38 and 52 px. Tracking is so tight that word spaces collapse: "at a time" reads close to "ata time", and "Friday release" and "stop three times" sit tight. The chart also stops at x 832 while the headline runs to 985, which leaves a dead band at the right. -> Put the 7, 7, 5 in their own right-aligned column (or left of the boxes at one x), set word-spacing back to a normal 0.25 em, and widen the boxes or the gap to the ceiling so the chart spans the headline's measure. (Judgement.)
3. page-01, the nine ghost slots (dashed boxes at x 610-800, rows y 668-882): they are the thinnest marks on the page (about 2 px dashes, 0.7 px at 360 px wide) and they carry the whole argument (empty room up to 9). At phone width the three rows read as 7, 7 and 5 solid boxes and the ceiling idea is lost; the 36 px source line (12 px at 360) is also at the edge. -> Draw the ghost slots as 4 px solid outlines at 40 percent ink, or as filled pale squares, so each row visibly shows 9 places; raise the source line to 40 px. (Rule for the source line: phone-width legibility; judgement for the slots.)
4. whole page: distinct judged against the specimen it started from and the tells clusters; no earlier pieces given. It departs from the specimen-label picture on every axis (no dark mount, no pinned sheet, no label card, no blue rule, no mono catalogue line) and it avoids all five clusters (no cream and clay, no near-black and acid, no broadsheet, no card kit, no eyebrows or mono chrome). The cost is that the "label" is now only the row captions: the dealt direction is not recognisable, and the page is the common layout of a big bold headline, a small chart, a pivot line and a source line. -> Give the three rows one real label form (a caption block with a rule, the way the specimen's caption sits under its object) so the direction shows in the chart and not only in the colours.
5. page-01, "One pause holds 9" (x 478-832, y 565-620) and the headline "seven at a time" (could improve, rule on precision): the post says one pause holds 5 to 9 and that the lists are "nine at most"; the page states 9 as what a pause holds, and "seven at a time" sits above a row that shows 5. Both are read as maxima in the post but as exact values here. -> "One pause holds 9 at most" and "seven at most, one pause at a time", or leave the headline and fix only the label.
