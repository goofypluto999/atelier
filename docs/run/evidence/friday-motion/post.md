The WHO surgical safety checklist has 19 checks, and the team never reads them in one go. It stops three times: 7 checks at sign in, 7 at time out, 5 at sign out. Atul Gawande's rule in The Checklist Manifesto is 5 to 9 items at a pause point, so one pause holds nine at most.

Cut a 19-check Friday release list the same way: 7 before the change, 7 at go-live, 5 before you leave. Google's SRE book puts roughly 70% of outages down to changes in a live system, and its three answers fit those three stops: roll out in stages, find problems fast, roll back safely.

Sources: WHO Surgical Safety Checklist (2009). Atul Gawande, The Checklist Manifesto (2009), ch. 6. Google, Site Reliability Engineering (2016), Introduction.
