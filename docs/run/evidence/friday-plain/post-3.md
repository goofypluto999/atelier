What does a Friday release checklist really need?

Roughly seven in ten outages are due to changes in a live system. That is the Google SRE book's figure, and its answer has three parts: roll changes out in steps, find problems quickly, and roll back safely when they appear.

So the Friday list stops before the change: is the rollout staged, will we see a fault quickly, and is the way back written down and tried? Everything else on the list matters less than that one stop.

Source: Site Reliability Engineering (Google, O'Reilly 2016), Introduction (sre.google/sre-book/introduction).
